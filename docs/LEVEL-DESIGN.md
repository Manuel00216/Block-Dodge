# Level design

The first five Jungle levels are planned for the first playable milestone. Targets below are prototypes and need playtesting. Optional star objectives remain to be chosen and tested.

| Level | World | Initial goal | Introduction | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Jungle | Dodge 20 | Single slow blocks | Planned |
| 2 | Jungle | Dodge 25 | More spawn positions | Planned |
| 3 | Jungle | Dodge 30 | Pairs with a safe gap | Planned |
| 4 | Jungle | Dodge 30 | Warned faster blocks | Planned |
| 5 | Jungle | Dodge 35 | Combine learned patterns | Planned |

Levels 6–10 finish Jungle. Ice Land covers 11–20, Desert 21–30 and Volcano 31–40. Levels 10, 20, 30 and 40 conclude their worlds.
