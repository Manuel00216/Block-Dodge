# Level design

The first five Jungle levels are planned for the first playable milestone. Targets below are prototypes and need playtesting. Current bonus goals are to avoid the arena edges and make at least one close dodge. They need player testing and tuning.

| Level | World | Initial goal | Introduction | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Jungle | Dodge 20 | Single slow blocks | Prototype |
| 2 | Jungle | Dodge 25 | More spawn positions | Prototype |
| 3 | Jungle | Dodge 30 | Pairs with a safe gap | Prototype |
| 4 | Jungle | Dodge 30 | Faster blocks (warning cues planned) | Prototype |
| 5 | Jungle | Dodge 35 | Combine learned patterns | Prototype |

Levels 6–10 finish Jungle. Ice Land covers 11–20, Desert 21–30 and Volcano 31–40. Levels 10, 20, 30 and 40 conclude their worlds.
